About the project

MindPulse AI is a machine learning demonstration project exploring patterns in student social media usage and mental health-related scores. The prediction comes from a regression model trained on a student dataset and served through a FastAPI backend.

It has not been clinically validated or approved for medical use. Treat results as an educational illustration of how a model responds to inputs, not as guidance about your health.
